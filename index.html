Our Story

More than a meal —
a true feast

Nestled in the verdant outskirts of Erode, Thottathu Virundhu was born from a simple belief: that food cooked with honest ingredients, in the way our grandmothers cooked, is the highest form of hospitality.

In Tamil, Virundhu means a feast prepared for guests — not just a meal, but an act of love and welcome. We honour this tradition with every dish that leaves our kitchen, sourcing locally grown produce, avoiding preservatives, and following age-old Kongunadu recipes.

From our outdoor cottage seating under the open sky to the aroma of slow-cooked Naattu Kozhi, every visit feels like coming home to a village kitchen.
